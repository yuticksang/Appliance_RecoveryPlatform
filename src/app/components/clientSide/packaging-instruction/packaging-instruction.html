Packaging Instructions

Packaging Instructions: Please follow these comprehensive steps to ensure your appliance is safely prepared for pickup. Proper packaging protects your appliance during transit and helps us provide an accurate valuation.

1 Safety First

  • Disconnect Power: Unplug the appliance from the electrical outlet at least 24 hours before pickup.
  • Turn Off Water/Gas: For appliances with water connections (washing machines, dishwashers, refrigerators with ice makers) or gas connections (dryers, ovens), shut off and disconnect all supply lines.
  • Allow Cooling: Let appliances cool completely if they generate heat (ovens, dryers, water heaters).
  • Defrost: Refrigerators and freezers must be fully defrosted and dried to prevent water leakage during transit.

2 Preparation Steps

  • Clean Thoroughly: Wipe down all interior and exterior surfaces. Remove any food residue, lint, or debris.
  • Remove Accessories: Take out all removable parts like shelves, racks, drawers, and trays. Wrap them separately or secure them inside the appliance.
  • Secure Moving Parts: Tape down doors, lids, and any moving components to prevent damage during transport.
  • Drain Water: Ensure all water is drained from washing machines, dishwashers, and water heaters. Check drain hoses and filters.

3 Protective Packaging

  • Corner Protection: Use foam padding or cardboard on all corners to prevent dents and scratches.
  • Wrap the Appliance: Cover the entire appliance with moving blankets, bubble wrap, or protective plastic sheeting.
  • Secure with Tape: Use strong packing tape to hold protective materials in place, but avoid taping directly onto painted or delicate surfaces.
  • Label Fragile Areas: Mark any particularly delicate parts (glass doors, control panels) with "FRAGILE" labels.

⚠️ Important: Do not use duct tape directly on the appliance surface as it may leave residue or damage the finish.

4 Final Preparation

  • Clear Pathway: Ensure there is a clear path from the appliance location to the pickup point. Remove obstacles, rugs, and any tripping hazards.
  • Measure Doorways: Verify that the appliance can fit through all doorways and hallways. Remove doors if necessary.
  • Accessibility: The appliance should be easily accessible on the pickup date. If it's in a basement or upper floor, inform us in advance.
  • Documentation: Have your recovery slip and any relevant documents ready for the pickup team.

📦 Ready for Pickup! Once you've completed these steps, your appliance is ready for our recovery team. Thank you for helping us ensure a smooth and safe pickup process!
