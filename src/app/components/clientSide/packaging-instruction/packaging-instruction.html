Packaging Instructions

Prepare your appliance for pickup

Packaging Instructions: Please follow these steps to ensure your appliance is safely prepared for pickup:

  1. Wrap fragile parts (e.g., control panels, knobs) with bubble wrap or soft padding.
  2. Secure the door or lid with strong tape to prevent it from opening during transit.
  3. Print the recovery slip and place together with the appliance in a sturdy box.
  4. Label the package with "Fragile" if applicable.
